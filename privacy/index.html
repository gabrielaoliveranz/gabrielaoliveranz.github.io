Privacy policy

Last updated: 4 November 2026

THE SHORT VERSION

I only ask for what I need.Your name, email and message when you get in touch, and the business data your project needs.
AI helps with the method, never with your data.I use AI tools for technical work like formulas and code. Your data never goes into them, is never sold or shared for marketing, and is only used for your project.
It's deleted within 30 days.Client data is deleted within 30 days of your project or plan ending.
You stay in control.You can ask to see, correct or delete what I hold about you at any time.

01Who I am

I'm Gabriela Olivera, a data analyst based in Mount Maunganui, New Zealand. In this policy, "I" and "me" means me, and "you" means anyone who visits this website, gets in touch, or works with me. I follow the Privacy Act 2020 and its Information Privacy Principles.

If you have any question about your information, email me at hello@gabrielaolivera.nz.

02What I collect

WHEN YOU GET IN TOUCH

Your name, your email address and anything you write in your message. If you choose to tell me about your business, I keep that too.

WHEN YOU JOIN THE NEWSLETTER

Your email address, and your first name if you give it, along with the date you joined. I only use it to send you Plain Numbers, and you can unsubscribe at any time using the link in every email.

Like most newsletter services, MailerLite records whether each email is opened and which links in it are clicked, using a small image in the email and tracked links. Links to this website also carry a short tag so I can see how many visits came from the newsletter. I use this to understand which topics are useful, never to build a profile of you, and I never share it. Most email apps let you turn off images, which stops open tracking.

If you reply to an issue, I keep your reply like any other message (see section 07). I may use common questions to shape future issues, but I never quote you or name you without your permission.

WHEN YOU WORK WITH ME

The business records you send for your project, such as sales, customer, product, supplier or delivery data. These records may include personal information about other people, for example your customers' names. I also keep the details I need to run the project and invoice you.

WHEN YOU VISIT THIS WEBSITE

This website does not set cookies of its own. To understand which pages are useful, I use GoatCounter, a privacy-friendly analytics service that counts visits without cookies and without identifying you. It records things like the page visited, the referring website, and your browser, screen size and country, not who you are.

The newsletter sign-up box on the home page is provided by MailerLite. It loads from MailerLite's servers and may use cookies or similar technologies to make the form work.

The website is hosted on GitHub Pages. Like any web host, GitHub may log technical details such as your IP address to keep the service secure. I don't have access to those logs.

03How I use it

  • To reply to your message and tell you which plan fits.
  • To deliver the work you asked for: cleaning, analysing and reporting on your data.
  • To invoice you and keep the records the law requires.
  • To send you Plain Numbers if you join, and to see which issues are useful.
  • To publish a case study, only with your written approval (see section 08).

I don't sell your information, I don't add you to any marketing list unless you choose to join the newsletter, and your data is never entered into AI tools.

I use AI tools for technical work, such as writing formulas and code, with made-up sample data only. If a future service would need AI to work with your data (for example, an automation project), that would only happen with your written agreement, setting out the tool used and how your data is handled.

04Your clients' and customers' information

When your records include personal information about other people, I hold it on your behalf, only for your project. Your business remains responsible for that information under the Privacy Act, and I only use it the way you've asked me to.

05How I keep it safe

  • Before we start, I set up a private Microsoft OneDrive folder just for your project. Files are shared through it, not as loose email attachments.
  • The folder is protected with two-step verification.
  • My laptop uses full-disk encryption and up-to-date antivirus software.
  • Only I have access to your project data.

06Who else sees it

Nobody, except the services I use to run my business, and only as far as they need to:

  • Formspree, which delivers messages from the contact form on this website to my inbox.
  • Microsoft (OneDrive, Excel and Power BI), to store and work on your project files.
  • GoatCounter, for the cookie-free visit counts described in section 02.
  • MailerLite, which stores newsletter subscribers, sends the newsletter emails and records opens and clicks.
  • Google Drive, which hosts the free Delivery Cost Checker you can download after joining the newsletter.

I would only share information with anyone else if the law requires it. Some of these services store information on servers outside New Zealand. I choose providers with strong security protections, and I take reasonable steps to make sure your information is protected in a way that is comparable to New Zealand law.

07How long I keep it

  • Project data: deleted within 30 days of your project finishing. For ongoing plans, within 30 days of the plan ending.
  • Newsletter subscribers: kept until you unsubscribe.
  • Messages from the contact form, and replies to the newsletter: kept for 12 months, then deleted, unless we start working together.
  • Invoices and business records: kept for 7 years, as New Zealand tax law requires.

08Case studies

Nothing about your project is published without your written approval. Any case study changes all figures and the names of your customers, suppliers and other private details, and your business name appears only if you choose.

09Your rights

You can ask to see the personal information I hold about you, ask me to correct it, or ask me to delete it. Email hello@gabrielaolivera.nz and I'll reply within 20 working days, usually much sooner.

If you're not happy with how I've handled your information, please tell me first so I can put it right. You can also contact the Office of the Privacy Commissioner at privacy.org.nz.

10If something goes wrong

If a privacy breach happens that could cause serious harm, I will tell the people affected and the Office of the Privacy Commissioner as soon as practicable, as the Privacy Act requires.

11Changes to this policy

If I change this policy, I'll update the date at the top. If a change affects how I handle a current client's data, I'll tell them directly.

Questions about this policy? Email hello@gabrielaolivera.nz.
