The questions this usually starts with

Most of the time, the answer is already sitting in your own numbers. It just hasn't been pulled together yet.

  • Are sales actually growing, or does it just feel that way?

    The real trend behind the headline number, once seasonality and one-off spikes are stripped out.

  • Which customers have quietly started ordering less?

    An early warning on customers drifting away, before they're gone for good.

  • Which products or customers are actually making you money?

    Profitability by product and by customer, not just top-line revenue.

  • Why is delivery costing more than it used to?

    Cost per order, per route and per customer, so you can see exactly where it's coming from.

How I can help

  • Data Health Check

    A first look at what data you already have (Xero, spreadsheets, POS, whatever you're using) and what shape it's in. You get a plain-English summary of what's usable now, what's missing, and what it would take to get real answers out of it.

  • Insight Dashboard

    A simple, ongoing view of the numbers that matter to your business (sales, margins, customers, delivery costs), easy to refresh each month with your latest data.

  • Custom Analysis

    A specific question, answered properly, such as "why did margins drop last quarter" or "which customers should we call before we lose them". A focused piece of analysis with a clear answer, not a generic report.
