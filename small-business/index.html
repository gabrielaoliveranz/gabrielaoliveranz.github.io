    Privacy and security

    Your data stays yours.

    • Your data is never entered into AI tools.

    • A private folder, just for your project.

      Before we start, I set up a private Microsoft OneDrive folder just for your project. Everything is shared through it, not loose email attachments.

    • Protected at every step.

      My laptop is protected with up-to-date antivirus software and full-disk encryption, and your OneDrive folder is protected with two-step verification.

    • Deleted within 30 days.

      Your data is deleted within 30 days of the project finishing. For ongoing plans, that's within 30 days of the plan ending.

    • In line with the Privacy Act 2020.

      Everything is handled in line with the Privacy Act 2020.

    • Nothing published without your written approval.

      Any case study changes all figures and the names of your customers, suppliers and other private details, and your business name appears only if you choose.
