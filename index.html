FAQs

Your questions, answered.

Something else on your mind? Ask me

What data do I need to send?

Usually exports you already have: sales by item or customer from Xero, and the spreadsheets you keep. Before we start, I send you a checklist of exactly what to pull together and where to find it.

Do I need Xero?

No. Xero exports make things easier, but spreadsheets and CSV exports from other systems work too. I work with your data in its existing format.

My data is messy. Is that a problem?

No. Cleaning and consolidating your data is part of every plan. Handwritten or photographed paper records need data entry first, which is quoted separately.

How long does it take?

Once I have all your data, first findings within a week and the final dashboard within 2 weeks. A Growth Dashboard, built in Power BI with a forecast, can take up to 4 weeks. A month after handover, I check in to make sure it's still answering your questions.

How much does it cost?

Every plan has a fixed price, confirmed in writing before any work starts. It depends on how many data sources you send and how much preparation they need.

What software will I need?

Excel for the Snapshot Report and Quarterly Check-in. The Growth Dashboard is built in Power BI, and you get the file or a PDF and video walkthrough.

Can I cancel an ongoing plan?

Yes, any time, with written notice.

What's not included?

Bookkeeping, accounting, tax, legal or financial advice, data entry of paper records, software licences and hardware.
